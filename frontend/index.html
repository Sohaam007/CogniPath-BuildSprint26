What is Alzheimer's?

Alzheimer's is a progressive neurologic disorder that causes brain cells to die. It is the most common cause of dementia, leading to a continuous decline in memory, thinking, and social skills.

Symptoms & Warning Signs

Early symptoms include forgetting recent events, misplacing items, getting lost in familiar places, confusion with time, and experiencing difficulty finding the right words or making decisions.

Prevention & Action

While there is no cure, early detection allows patients to intervene with lifestyle modifications (diet, cognitive exercises), enroll in clinical trials, and access emerging disease-modifying therapies.
